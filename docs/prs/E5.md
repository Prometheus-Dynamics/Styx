## Build

- Encode and decode through the Lemnos `codec-block` contract (rk-mpp, a733-video, hevc, m2m-decode): Styx parses or builds the bitstream and supplies the controls; Lemnos runs the block. No vendor library.
- Bitstream work is Styx's: the HEVC header parser and the stateless-decode logic (recovery included) move here from Lemnos `drivers/hevc` and `drivers/m2m-decode`; Lemnos keeps the M2M session (queues, buffers, requests). H.264 the same way.

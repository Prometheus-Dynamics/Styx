## Build

- Frames to other processes through the stack's one ring (Lemnos's leaf crates: ring, buffers handed over once, hold marks, doorbell, fd passing). The camera service hands each reader the buffers, the ring and a doorbell once over a Unix socket; readers hold by marks; the producer drops and never waits. No Orion needed.
- Per-reader limits (frames held, how long) and frame age measured and reported (dev's latest-only and queue depth).

## Proof

Tests: two readers, one slow; the slow one skips and counts; 0 copies; a dead reader's holds go away.

## Build

- The Lemnos `next` client and the camera-sensor, -receiver, -isp, -frames, codec-block, image-scaler and lens contracts, wrapped once for Styx: find devices by tag, follow `link`, open streams, map stream buffers into FrameLease without copying.
- Read Lemnos `docs/design.md` (Cameras, The frame stream) and `contracts/camera-*`. Use them as they are; record gaps in the PR text.
- Also the Lemnos library crates Styx uses without lemnosd: `lemnos-v4l2`, the ISP layout and tiling libraries (pisp, rkisp, k230isp, isp600, without `device`), netlink hotplug, the frame-stream mapping and the shared ring. One place, one lock.

## Proof

Tests against Lemnos's simulated backend: a sensor-receiver-ISP chain is found by tag and link; stream buffers become FrameLeases with 0 copies.

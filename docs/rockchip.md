# Rockchip (RK3588 / RK3588S)

Status: groundwork. `styx-rkisp` holds the ISP30's parameter and statistics buffers and
converts between them and Styx's 3A; nothing has run on an RK3588 yet (no board on the bench).
The NEON software ISP already runs on any Rockchip ARM board.

## Why Styx can drive the ISP itself

- **Mainline Linux:** the `rkcif` capture driver landed in 6.19 (PX30, RK3568), with RK3588
  capture following. The RK35-generation ISPs (ISP21 in RK3566/68, ISP30 in RK3588(S), the
  RK3576's) have no mainline driver; a from-scratch `rkisp2` is planned upstream.
- **Rockchip's BSP kernel (5.10 / 6.1):** the `rkisp` driver is GPL source, and its parameter
  and statistics buffers are defined in uAPI headers licensed
  `(GPL-2.0+ WITH Linux-syscall-note) OR MIT` (`rk-isp3-config.h` and the `rk-isp21` /
  `rk-isp2` headers it includes). A parameter buffer per frame, a statistics buffer back: the
  same pattern as the PiSP. Rockchip's own 3A (`rkaiq`, partly prebuilt) is not needed:
  Styx's 3A (`styx-algo`) writes the parameters, as it does on the Pi.
- A product on this path runs the vendor kernel until `rkisp2` lands; the parameter and
  statistics backend then follows the mainline interface.

## styx-rkisp

- `uapi`: the ISP30 layouts, generated by `scripts/rkisp-bindgen.sh` from the headers in
  `crates/rkisp/headers`; their sizes (10921-byte parameters, 16172-byte statistics) and the
  offsets of the blocks used are checked against the C compiler's at compile time. The
  module bits of `module_ens` are written out by hand (`uapi::module`).
- `Params`: one frame's parameters: the statistics Styx reads (`measure`: the big raw AE
  block's 15x15 zone means, the big raw histogram of luma), black level, white balance gains,
  colour matrix, output gamma.
- `statistics`: a statistics buffer into `styx_algo::Statistics` (colour zones and histogram,
  before white balance and lens shading).

The fixed-point formats the headers do not state are named constants taken from how
Rockchip's 3A fills the same fields, to be confirmed on the device: white balance gains with 8
fractional bits (`GAIN_ONE`), matrix coefficients with 7 (`CCM_ONE`), 12-bit gamma output
(`GAMMA_OUT_MAX`), AE zone means of 10 bits for red and blue and 12 for green (`AE_RB_FULL`,
`AE_G_FULL`).

## Next, with an RK3588S board

1. Capture: the sensor (OV9782, 2-lane CSI-2, RAW8) through `rkcif` and the ISP's media graph;
   the parameter and statistics video nodes (`rkisp-input-params`, `rkisp-statistics`).
2. A native backend that queues `Params` per frame and feeds `statistics` to the 3A loop, as
   the PiSP path does; confirm the fixed-point constants against captured frames.
3. Lens shading, denoise and sharpening blocks; tuning from the ColorChecker session.
4. Up to four cameras on one SoC: the two ISP30 units time-shared between cameras (the vendor
   driver's multi-camera mode).

The RK3582 (a cut-down RK3588S, pin and package identical) keeps the ISP30 and four 2-lane
CSI-2 ports per its datasheet, so the same backend covers it. RK3576 (another ISP generation) is a fallback; RK3566 is ruled out.
